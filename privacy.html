Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Still Here, why it's collected, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

This site does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or payment details, and does not have a login form. Any reading you do here is anonymous.

2. Cookies and local storage

The site stores a small amount of data in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage. This is used only to remember interface state — for example, whether you have already been shown a particular screen — so the site behaves consistently between visits. It is never sent to us and cannot identify you.

You can clear this at any time through your browser settings under "Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data".

3. Advertising

This site displays advertising served by a third-party advertising network — at present Adsterra. That network may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Ads are delivered inside embedded frames (iframes). The site itself does not read, store, or pass on anything that happens inside those frames.
  • You can see which vendors are active on this and other sites, and opt out of personalised advertising, at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Where Google advertising cookies are also involved, you may opt out at Google Ads Settings.

Network policies: Adsterra privacy policy · How Google uses cookies.

4. Third-party content

QR codes shown on the support screen are generated by a third-party service (api.qrserver.com) and contain only the public wallet address displayed on screen. No personal data is transmitted with that request.

5. Donations

Donations are voluntary and are sent directly from your wallet to a public blockchain address shown on the site. We never see, receive, or store your wallet address, keys, or transaction history on any server. Blockchain transactions are public by design and are governed by the privacy policy of the network you use.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and similar regulations you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Because this site does not collect personal data, we are unlikely to hold any — but you may still contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit the contact page.
