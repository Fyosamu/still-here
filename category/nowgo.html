🚀 NOW GO

Twenty-five short instructions for the moment after reading. Start embarrassingly small, feel it and do it anyway, track it or it didn't happen. Nothing here asks for a personality transplant.

Advertisement
  1. 101The gene doesn't care how you feelGood news. Neither should you, about the excuses you've been rehearsing.
  2. 102Motivation is a guest, not a tenantIt'll visit sometimes. Don't hand it the lease. Do the work while it's out of the house.
  3. 103Start embarrassingly smallTwo minutes. One sentence. One push-up. The bar has to be low enough that arguing with it looks stupid.
  4. 104Nobody's comingNot a mentor, not a moment, not a sign from the universe. The only sign you're getting is the one you build yourself.
  5. 105The discomfort is the whole productEverything you want is sitting on the other side of something you don't want to do. Every time.
  6. 106You're allowed to be bad at itFor a while. Being bad at something is just the entry fee, and everybody pays it in full, upfront.
  7. 107Discipline beats feelingsFeelings change four times before lunch. Discipline is just feelings that agreed to show up anyway.
  8. 108Your ancestors survived worse with lessA famine, a war, a winter with no heater. Your excuse is a push notification.
  9. 109Comparison is a rigged gameYou're putting your chapter two next to their chapter twenty, using their highlight reel as the ruler.
  10. 110Stop negotiating with 6 a.m.Decide the night before. The morning version of you is not to be trusted with anything important.
  11. 111Boredom is a superpowerCan't sit still anymore? Nobody can. The one person who still can is going to take all of it.
  12. 112Do the boring thing unimpressivelyNot perfectly, not with a color-coded planner. Just done, badly, again tomorrow. That's the whole method.
  13. 113You don't need a breakthroughYou need four hundred unremarkable days in a row and the willingness to not be excited about most of them.
  14. 114The work is the rewardNot eventually, actually. The day you stop dreading it is the exact day it starts paying you back.
  15. 115You're behind on a clock you inventedThere's no schedule. There's no deadline. There's just people who started and people who are still thinking about it.
  16. 116Nobody's keeping scoreWhich is both freeing and terrifying. Nobody will notice if you quit. That's exactly why you shouldn't.
  17. 117Quitting is quietSuccess makes noise. Failure just closes a tab one afternoon and nobody ever asks what happened there.
  18. 118Make it stupidly easy to startShoes by the door. Document already open. Remove the friction and you remove most of the argument.
  19. 119Track it or it didn't happenOne line a day. You'll hate the empty days far more than you hate the actual work.
  20. 120Rest is part of the planNot a betrayal of the plan. A machine that never stops isn't dedicated — it's just broken and hasn't told anyone.
  21. 121Your mood is weatherIt passes whether you argue with it or not. Work through it and it becomes just a Tuesday that happened to rain.
  22. 122Feel it and do it anywayThat's not suppression, that's adulthood. The fear stays exactly where it is. You move regardless.
  23. 123Five years from now is coming either wayYou'll arrive at it no matter what you do. Might as well have something to show for the trip.
  24. 124The you of next year is watchingNot mystically. Just practically — they're either going to be grateful or annoyed, and you're the only one who picks…
  25. 125You're the only person who canNot the most talented, not the most connected. Just the only one with your exact access to right now.
