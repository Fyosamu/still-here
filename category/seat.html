✨ YOU GOT A SEAT

Twenty-five reasons, each about a minute long, that what you have is unusual. Not cheerful noise: the actual odds, the actual timeline, and the small abilities you use without noticing.

  1. 051Infinite people never got hereEvery line that ended, every person who never reproduced, every road that just stopped. You're the exception, not the…
  2. 052And infinite more won'tPeople who'll never be born because of a choice made in the next five minutes. You're not late. You're not early…
  3. 053The seat was almost not yoursOne different decision by one ancestor and you're a different person, or no person at all.
  4. 054You're alive right nowNot in a postcard way. Actually. While you read this, you're one of the tiny fraction of everything that can notice…
  5. 055The universe is 13.8 billion years oldIt waited that long to build something that could look back at it. That something is currently you, on a screen…
  6. 056You can taste thingsA structure made of stardust figured out how to detect sugar. Nothing else up there has that feature.
  7. 057You can get boredComplaining about boredom is a luxury problem. Rocks don't get bored. Neither does 99.9999% of everything that exists.
  8. 058You can miss peopleMissing requires memory, which requires a brain that bothered to store something. Amazing machine, terrible billing…
  9. 059Someone laughed at something you said onceYou probably don't remember it. They might. That's a real event in a real universe, caused directly by you.
  10. 060The weather is freeNot money-free. Just: nobody charged you for the color of that sunset, and it still happened anyway.
  11. 061You have a tomorrowNot guaranteed, but scheduled. Most things that ever existed had no schedule at all, not even a rough one.
  12. 062You can change your mindGenuinely. A single cell can't. A rock can't. You can read one card and walk away different than you arrived.
  13. 063Someone from 500 years ago would call you a godRunning water, a light you switch on with your finger, food from three continents. You've been bored of miracles for…
  14. 064You're bored of miraclesLet that land properly. You got handed the impossible and filed it under "Tuesday, nothing special."
  15. 065You can choose what to think about nextThat wasn't always on. For most of history, most people got one job and one set of rules and no say in the matter.
  16. 066You're not starving while reading thisIf you are — eat first, come back. If you're not, that's one problem already solved today. Check.
  17. 067You have access to every book ever writtenIn your pocket, for free. And we mostly use it to watch a stranger fall off a chair.
  18. 068That's still your rightNobody has to earn the right to waste an afternoon. Sometimes wasting the afternoon is the entire point of the…
  19. 069You can be kind on purposeInstinct gets you some of it. Doing it when it's inconvenient — that's the part no gene ever asked for.
  20. 070You can forgive a geneYours is running on very old code with a very short memory. Knowing that makes you the adult in the room.
  21. 071The people after you aren't born yetSomeone in 2140 will exist because of something you do or don't do. You're not a passenger. You're the hinge.
  22. 072Someone before you gave up something for youA name you'll never know stayed alive long enough for your line to continue. They get no credit. You are the credit.
  23. 073It cost 13.8 billion years to get you hereThat's the bill on the table. Try not to undersell the product.
  24. 074Nobody's handing out medals for existingWhich is strange, because existing was genuinely the hard part. Everything after that is negotiable.
  25. 075So what are you doing with it?Not a demand. Just a real question. You've got the rarest thing in the cosmos and it's on a timer.
