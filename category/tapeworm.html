🦧 GLAD YOU'RE NOT THIS

Twenty-five animals with no mouth, no stomach, no eyes or no plan — every one of them alive, successful, and having a worse time than you. The category works as a low bar. You clear it by breathing.

  1. 076The tapewormNo mouth. No stomach. No eyes. No brain. It lives in the dark inside another creature and absorbs whatever it's…
  2. 077That's the barHalf a billion years of what we'd call "success," and it has never once gotten to see anything at all.
  3. 078It has no idea it existsAnd honestly, neither do you most days. The difference is that you get to notice that, which is somehow worse and way…
  4. 079Nobody wrote a poem about a tapewormAnd it wouldn't matter if they did. There's no one in there to read it.
  5. 080Its whole life is someone else's lunchIt never picked a meal, a room, a name. It just arrived inside a system and did the only thing it knows how to do.
  6. 081Survival with zero enjoymentYou say life is hard. A tapeworm couldn't understand the complaint, or the concept of "better," or the word "worse."
  7. 082And it's still winningIt outlived the dinosaurs. It'll probably outlive us. Which should tell you that winning was never the point.
  8. 083Now the parasitic waspIt stings a caterpillar, paralyzes it, lays eggs inside, and the eggs hatch and eat the host from the inside out…
  9. 084That's a Tuesday for itNo villain. No plan. Just a program running with the brightness turned all the way off.
  10. 085The botfly larvaBurrows into skin, eats its way around, comes out when it's finished. You'd be screaming the entire time. It wouldn't…
  11. 086The male anglerfishHe bites the female, fuses to her, dissolves, and becomes nothing but a pair of gonads. That's the entire career path.
  12. 087Koala fingerprintsUnder a microscope they're indistinguishable from a human's. Evolution ran out of ideas and copied ours. Nobody asked…
  13. 088Your cat would eat you if you stopped movingNot out of malice. Its "care" setting simply doesn't cover you. You are not in the config file.
  14. 089The point isn't "it's worse out there"The point is: you got the version with eyes, jokes, cold drinks, and the ability to be annoyed by all three.
  15. 090You can be sad. They can't even get that far.Sadness requires a self to be sad about. Most living things never make it as far as having a self.
  16. 091Being miserable is an upgradeRead that again with a straight face. It means you have something to lose, which is not a small thing.
  17. 092A rock isn't depressedIt also isn't anything. The minimum entry fee for being anything at all is that you sometimes feel bad about it.
  18. 093The tapeworm can't have a bad dayIt also can't have a good one. That's the trade it made by not bothering with a brain.
  19. 094You get bothGood day, bad day, weird day, day you refuse to describe to anyone. Both columns come with the same membership.
  20. 095Nobody has ever envied a jellyfishNo brain, no heart, no blood. It drifts. It's extremely good at drifting. Still not what anyone's aiming for.
  21. 096Consider the spongeIt doesn't eat, think, move, or notice anything. It's been at this for 600 million years. Not one person is jealous.
  22. 097Glad you're not that?Then you already answered what you'd rather be instead. Hold onto that answer, it's more useful than it looks.
  23. 098Every alternative on the table is worseThat's not a reason to be smug. It's a reason to stop renegotiating with your own life.
  24. 099You drew the only hand that can complain about the handWhich is either the universe's best joke or its entire point, and nobody's confirming which.
  25. 100So no, you don't want to be a tapewormGood. Now go act like somebody who knows exactly what they dodged.
