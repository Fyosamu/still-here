## Long — for "tell us more" (about 1200)

```
STILL HERE is a free, installable Progressive Web App: 150 short reads, roughly a minute
each, written for people who want perspective without a course, a coach or a sign-up.

WHAT IT IS
Six categories of 25 reads each. THE NUMBERS works from concrete figures — a $30,000
apartment, a $120 month — and follows them to the conclusion instead of stopping at the
complaint. THE BIOLOGY OF YOU traces a person back to a single cell and a gene that only
wants to be copied, which turns out to explain more about daily emotions than most
self-help does. YOU GOT A SEAT is a perspective category: the odds, the timeline, the
things you can do that almost nothing else in the universe can. GLAD YOU'RE NOT THIS is
a nature category built around one animal per read — tapeworm, botfly larva, parasitic
wasp, male anglerfish — used as a low bar you can clear by accident. NOW GO is the
practical category: start embarrassingly small, discipline over mood, track it or it
didn't happen. LOOSE THOUGHTS is everything that refused to be categorised.

HOW IT WORKS
It is a PWA. There is nothing to install from a store — open it once, use the browser's
"Add to Home Screen" and it appears as an app with its own icon, its own window and an
offline cache. No account, no email, no onboarding.

EVERY READ IS ALSO A PAGE
Because each of the 150 reads has its own URL, title and description, any single one can
be shared as a link that previews properly and reads without the app around it.

BUSINESS MODEL
One banner ad on a card click and a donation button pointing at a public wallet. Nothing
is sold, nothing is collected, there is no analytics profile of the reader.

Built and maintained by one person. Free forever.
```

**Subject**


**Body**

```
Hi — I made a free web app I think your readers would like.

STILL HERE (https://fyosamu.github.io/still-here/) is 150 short reads, about a minute
each: what a $120 month actually implies, where your opinions come from, why you got a
seat at all, and one animal per read you should be grateful you're not.

It installs like an app, works offline, and never asks for an email.

A single read to look at first:
https://fyosamu.github.io/still-here/c/76.html

Happy to send screenshots or a short description in your format. Thanks for the time.

— Fyosamu
```

Send it to *one* person at a time, and change the first line so it is obviously about
their publication. Batch-sent identical emails are what gets a domain marked as spam.

## Screenshots to attach

Directories want 3–5 images. Capture these (portrait, phone width):

1. The home screen with the six categories
2. The reader open on a card — `?c=76` is the tapeworm, the most striking one
3. The category grid — `?c=0`
4. The install prompt / home-screen icon
5. `og.png` where a wide image is required (1200×630)

Never submit the pin artwork as an app screenshot — it is marketing art, not the app, and
directories reject that.
